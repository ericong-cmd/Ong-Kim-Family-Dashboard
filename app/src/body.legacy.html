Financial-year boundaries & components

Opening basis. Market value — what the portfolio was worth at the close of the last day of the previous month (31 Mar for a 1 Apr start, 31 Dec for 1 Jan). You supply it from a broker valuation; it gives a true return for the period, annualised as XIRR. Cost basis — what the holdings cost when bought, worked out from the ledger with no input needed; it shows gain accumulated since purchase, so it is not a period return and is not annualised. Closing left blank values the portfolio at current prices. Boundary settings are stored per period & owner. Opening balances (OPENING records) inside the year count as capital already in place, not a cash flow.

Field guide
BUY / SELL / OPENING use quantity & price. Buy fees are capitalised into cost; opening balances carry your cost basis in “price / share”. DIVIDEND takes a total amount, not qty × price. CORPORATE ACTION rescales remaining lots (2 : 1 split → “2 : 1”; 1-for-10 bonus → “11 : 10”).

How “Pull live prices” works
A browser file can't reach IBKR directly, so the button bridges through the Claude connector. In a standalone file it builds a request naming every held ticker (with its IBKR contract id), copies it to your clipboard, and shows it — paste it to Claude, who pulls the quotes and returns an updated price block to Restore JSON. Opened as a Cowork live view instead, it calls the connector directly and writes the prices straight in. Either way it's refresh-on-demand, not a ticking feed; real-time vs ~15-min delayed depends on your IBKR market-data subscription, and outside SGX hours the latest daily close is used.
