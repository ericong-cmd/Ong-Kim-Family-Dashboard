Period settings — opening basis & boundary values
Market value — the portfolio's worth at the close of the day before the period starts (31 Mar for 1 Apr, 31 Dec for 1 Jan); gives a true period return, annualised as XIRR for completed years. Cost basis — what the holdings cost, worked out from the ledger; shows gain accumulated since purchase, not a period return. Closing left blank values the portfolio at current prices. Settings are stored per period & owner.

Field guide
BUY / SELL / OPENING use quantity & price. Buy fees are capitalised into cost; opening balances carry your cost basis in “price / share”. DIVIDEND takes a total amount, not qty × price. CORPORATE ACTION rescales remaining lots (2 : 1 split → “2 : 1”; 1-for-10 bonus → “11 : 10”).
